# How chapter pictures work

Notes for whoever changes the Illustrate page (`src/app/c/[id]/illustrate/page.tsx`) or the cover page (`src/app/s/[id]/cover/page.tsx`), and the file to read before touching them. All ComfyUI calls are made from the browser, never from the StoryTime server. Planned work is in `TODO.md`; how to set up ComfyUI is in `docs/pictures.md`.

- **Targets.** Pictures are made for a target (`PictureTarget` in `src/lib/pictureTarget.ts`), a union with a `kind`: `chapter`, `cover` (the story's) or `codex` (an entry, `entryId`). The queue, job tags, file prefix, browser storage (`stateKey`), saver and Pictures panel all take one, and ask it rather than checking its kind: `slotsFor` (the chapter's header and footer, `cover`, or `codex`), `targetKey` (`chapter:<id>`, `cover:<story id>`, `codex:<entry id>`), `targetHref`, `presetApi`, `othersQuery`, `savedPictureApi`. The stored formats carry a chapter id that is null for cover and codex jobs (`chapterIdOf`, `targetOfIds`).
- **Codex pictures** (migration 25; `src/lib/codexPictures.ts`). An entry's picture can change over the story, on its branch only, like codex text: `codex_pictures` rows are anchored at a chapter (null = the whole story), apply to it and its descendants, and `codexPictureAt` / `codexPicturesAt` pick the deepest anchor on the root-to-chapter path, then the latest (`posted_at`); removing one only hides it (`removed_at`). APIs: `GET /api/stories/:id/codex-pictures?chapter=` (each entry's picture there, and `ids`), `&entry=` (all of an entry's pictures and the current one), `POST ?entry=&anchor=&job=&index=` (image body; saves and pins the job's picture), `GET/DELETE .../codex-pictures/:pictureId`, `GET/PUT /codex-draft?entry=` (`codex_picture_prompts`, with `refs`) and `/codex-preset?entry=`. A codex job is in `picture_jobs` with `entry_id` and the moment `codex` (no chapter); the cover's job lists leave them out, and `GET /api/picture-jobs?story=&entry=` lists an entry's. Its ComfyUI tag is `{chapterId: null, entryId, moment: "codex"}` (old tags without `entryId` still read as cover jobs) and its files go under `StoryTime/<story id>/codex/<entry id>`. The page `/s/<id>/codex/<entry>` (`?at=<chapter id>`: the chapter the author came from, whose text and picture it shows) is `CodexWorkspace`: `CodexPromptPanel` (the prompt is written from the entry's text as of that chapter, `src/lib/ai/codexPrompt.ts`: one full-body picture of a character, the object, or an establishing picture of a place, on a plain background, sharing the tag and sentence rules and `PromptFields`) and `CodexPictureCard` as the destination. A picture's ☰ menu offers Use as codex picture, from the start, and (with `?at=`) from “<chapter>” on (`SaveAnchor` in `usePictureSaver`). The Codex panel (`CodexPanel`, given `storyId`) shows each entry's picture as of its chapter as a thumbnail and a Make a picture / Picture link to the page with `?at=`; the picture queue's list names the job “<entry name> (codex)” (`GET /api/chapters/labels?entries=`). Tests: `tests/e2e/codex-pictures.e2e.mjs`, `tests/api/codex-pictures.test.mjs`, `tests/unit/codex-prompt.test.mts`.
- **Codex references.** The References picker has a **Codex** tab: the entries with a picture as of the page's chapter (`RefPool.codex`, from `codexRefChoices`; on the cover page the whole-story pictures), added as Character, Item or Place by the entry's type with the entry's name as label and source `codex:<picture id>`. Suggested also lists an entry whose name or alias is in the prompt or What to show (whole word, any case; `mentions`). A kept `codex:` reference is restored while its picture exists (`RefPool.codexIds`).
- **The cover page** (`/s/<id>/cover`, linked as Make a cover from the Cover image card on the story's details page) is the Illustrate page for the story's cover: `CoverWorkspace` lays out the same columns with `CoverPromptPanel` (prompt, then `ComfyGenerate`) and, in the gallery, a `ChapterImageEditor` for the `cover` slot and `ChapterPictures` (its last view is Chapters: every chapter's pictures, `?story=&chapters=1`). The ☰ menu offers Use as cover. **Write from** says what the prompt is written from (`coverPromptRequest` in `src/lib/ai/coverPrompt.ts`, which shares the tags and sentence rules with chapter prompts): the summary and codex, the first chapter, or a path to a chosen chapter (the Path ends at menu lists the chapters in the outline's order; the path and its codex are loaded with `GET /api/chapters/:id` and `/codex`). A story with no chapters can only use the summary. The boxes, the source and the chosen chapter are kept in `cover_prompts` (migration 20, with the References as `refs` from migration 24; `src/lib/coverPictures.ts`; `GET/PUT /api/stories/:id/cover-draft`, saved by `useCoverDraft`), and so is the cover's own drawing preset (`/cover-preset`; null follows the story's). Cover pictures have their own size (`settings.comfy.coverWidth/coverHeight`, 832 × 1216 by default, with a Size menu of tall sizes first), so choosing one doesn't change the chapters'. The saved cover can be a starting picture (`saved:<story id>:cover`). Uploading a cover still works on the details page.
- **Components** (`src/components/`): `usePictureWorkspace` holds the state a picture page shares (the prompt loaded from a picture, the starting picture, the jobs being made, the column width, and all of the target's pictures as `Picture`s from `pictures.ts`: the page's jobs, then those made on this visit, marked `fresh`). `IllustrateWorkspace` uses it and lays out the two columns: `.illustrate-controls` (the `IllustrateChapter` panel, passed in by the page, then `ImagePromptPanel`: prompt, then `ComfyGenerate`) and `.illustrate-gallery` (the two `ChapterImageEditor` cards first, to compare new pictures with, then `ChapterPictures`). In one column (narrower than 64rem) the two `ChapterImageEditor` cards come first, above the chapter panel (CSS: the gallery is `display: contents` and the cards have `order: -1`). On a wide screen (64rem) the controls column is sticky and the page uses up to 120rem. The Size menu shows each option as columns: shape glyph, name, aspect ratio, pixels (`SizeColumns`, laid out by `.size-select` with the browser's customizable select, `appearance: base-select`, which needs an explicit `<button><selectedcontent>` child so the closed menu has the columns too; browsers without it show the same text in an ordinary menu). Beside the Size menu a **Scale by** box (a × multiplier; its label avoids the word "Size" so `getByLabel('Size')` still finds only the menu) (`settings.comfy.sizeScale`, 0.5 to 2, default 1, on the server and shared by chapters and the cover) scales the chosen size when a picture is asked for (`ComfyGenerate`'s `comfy` is the scaled size, `baseComfy` the menu's), rounded to a multiple of 16, with the result shown beside it; it is off while a starting picture is set, and Use its settings on a loaded picture resets it to 1. The story's art style is shown in small type under the Prompt box (`.prompt-art-style`), since it is added to every prompt. `ComfyGenerate` makes one picture per click by default; the Pictures per click menu beside Queue (`settings.comfy.queueCount`: 1, 2, 4, 5 or 8, on the server) makes one click call `ask()` that many times, so they gather into one job like quick clicks (there is no How many menu; `settings.comfy.count` is left over and unused): clicks with nothing changed, each within 0.8 s of the last (`GATHER_MS`), are gathered into one job of up to 8 pictures (`MAX_BATCH`), and a click with anything changed sends the gathered job at once and starts another. Several jobs can wait in ComfyUI's queue, each with the prompt and settings as they were when asked for. It hands the jobs being made up through `onQueue` (as `PendingJob`s, newest first, each with its `stop`) and each finished job's pictures through `onPictures`; `ChapterPictures` shows each job as a place kept for each of its pictures (`li.comfy-pending-tile` in the picture grid, at the size setting's shape, or the starting picture's once it is uploaded), with the job's progress drawn over each place (only the first is a `role=status`, and has Stop). Stop only interrupts a job ComfyUI's `/queue` says is running, since older ComfyUIs interrupt whatever is running.
- **The Pictures panel** (`ChapterPictures`) is a timeline, newest first by default: the jobs being made (newest first, so the one running is last, next to the newest pictures), then the pictures. The button beside Clear unpinned switches it to oldest first (`settings.comfy.picturesOldestFirst`, kept on the server so it is the same on every device): the runs, and the pictures inside each, are reversed and a job being made comes after its pictures, so the newest is at the bottom; the viewer follows the order shown. It does not apply to Other chapters, which is always newest first. `timeline()` in `pictures.ts` puts neighbours made with the same prompt, negative prompt, checkpoint, moment, starting picture, strength and whether a painted area was used (not which: a job being made doesn't know its mask's file name yet) (and size, from the prompt alone) in one group, so two batches in a row share a group and A, B, A is three groups; a job being made joins the newest group when its settings match. Groups have no heading, only a line between them (`.picture-group`'s top border); each group's list is named `Pictures from “<prompt>”` for screen readers (and the tests). Other chapters likewise shows only the chapter titles, with a line between jobs. Jobs being made join the groups in Pinned & new and All; in Rejected and Other chapters they show on their own at the top. Views (`inView` in `pictures.ts`): **Pinned & new** (default: pinned, plus this visit's unmarked ones, and those of jobs that finished while the page wasn't open, until Clear unpinned), **All** (everything not rejected), **Rejected**, and **Other chapters** (`OtherChapterPictures`: the story's other chapters, paged, through `?story=&not_chapter=`). So on return only pinned pictures show. Clear keeps nothing on the server. Each picture has a row of round buttons on its top right corner (`PictureButtons`, shared by `PictureTile` and the full-size viewer, so they look and sit the same in both): pin (a thumbtack, filled when pinned) and reject (✕, ↺ to restore), and a ☰ menu with Use as header picture and Use as footer picture (icons from `PictureIcons.tsx`; the slot it is in reads "This is the header picture" and is marked unavailable), **Instruct changes** (the picture becomes the Instruct starting picture and the page scrolls to the prompt; off, with the reason as its title, while no edit model is ready), Use as reference, Refine…, Load prompt, Info and Open in a new tab (`startItems` in `PictureButtons.tsx`; `StartRequest` in `pictures.ts` is `"instruct"`, `"reference"` or `"refine"`). A picture made from a painted area (an earlier one, or the Refine screen's) has “painted area” in its caption. **Info** (`PictureInfo`, a modal `<dialog>`, also over the viewer and for Other chapters' pictures) shows the prompt, negative prompt and `pictureDetails()` (chapter and moment, when made, checkpoint, size, seed, batch size and index, starting picture and strength, “Area: painted part only” when a mask was used, file), with Copy prompt, Copy negative prompt, Copy all as Markdown (`pictureMarkdown()`, prompts in code blocks), Load prompt, **Instruct changes** (the same as the ☰ item: it sets the Instruct starting picture) and Refine…. Copying works over plain http too (`copyText` falls back to a hidden text box, put inside the dialog because the page behind a modal can't be selected). The workflow's other settings (sampler, steps, CFG) aren't recorded, so Info can't show them. Choosing an item puts the keyboard back on ☰. A mark change saves at once, is put back if the server refuses, and offers Undo. The picture that is the chapter's header or footer now has a badge on its top left corner (here and in the viewer): `chapter_images.image_index` (migration 15) says which of the job's pictures was saved, sent as `PUT .../images/:slot?job=<id>&index=<n>`; one saved before that is only recognised when its job made a single picture. Clicking a picture opens `PictureViewer` (a modal `<dialog>`, whole screen) on it, going through the view's pictures in the order shown: ← → or a swipe, P pins, X rejects (a picture that leaves the view moves the viewer on), Esc closes (or closes the ☰ menu when it is open). In the viewer the picture sits in a frame of its own shape (`.viewer-frame`, sized with container units), so its buttons and badge are on its corners as on a tile. Ctrl/Cmd/Shift or middle click still opens the picture in a new tab. `usePictureSaver` saves a picture as the header or footer. **Temporary pictures** (see the job log) are hidden in every view, the counts, and for jobs being made, unless pinned (`hiddenTemporary`/`inView` in `pictures.ts`) or the checkbox “Include temporary inpainting images” above the pictures is on. The checkbox is off on every visit (plain state, not remembered) and only appears when the story has a temporary job a normal listing leaves out (asked of `?story=&temporary=hidden&limit=1` when the panel opens), or the page holds a temporary picture or job being made. Turning it on loads the target's temporary jobs (`temporary=only`) into the page's list (`addLoaded`) and adds `temporary=1` to Other chapters; temporary pictures say “temporary” in their caption and Info has a “Temporary” row. A temporary picture that is **pinned** (an accepted Refine result) is a kept picture and has neither: only unpinned temporary pictures, which show through the switch, are labelled (`pictureCaption` and `pictureDetails` look at `mark`).
- **The picture queue** is the app's one work queue (`WorkQueue.tsx`, `WorkQueueProvider` in the root layout, `useWorkQueue()`; `usePictureQueue()` is kept as a hook with the same API over it). Its items are a union tagged by `type` (not `kind`, which a picture job already has from its `PictureTarget`): `{type: "picture", ...}` for now (writing comes later). When to send a gathered job to ComfyUI is decided by the pure module `src/lib/workQueue/schedule.ts` (`nextActions`), which for now sends every ready job at once, in the order it became ready, so pictures behave as before. `schedule.ts` also has `orderHeldPictures(held, lastPreset, group)`, the rule for jobs the queue holds back in StoryTime (not used by the provider until the queue can hold jobs, task 1.4): jobs for the drawing preset of the last job sent go first, a target's own jobs keep their order. Its settings are `settings.queue` (Settings › Queue: `sharing` "turns" | "together", `switchMinutes`, `switchItems`, `groupPictures`; older settings read as the defaults; nothing reads them yet apart from the grouping rule's tests). **Pause and order:** `nextActions` sends nothing while paused (`WorkQueueApi.paused` / `setPaused`, kept in this browser under `localStorage["storytime-queue-paused"]`, so a reload stays paused; the header button shows “Paused”), and a held job waits in the provider's `ready` list, in the order `waitingOrder` gives (jobs the owner moved by hand first, then the rest grouped by `orderHeldPictures` using the preset of the last job sent and `settings.queue.groupPictures`). `move(key, "up" | "down" | "next")` uses `moveWaiting`: a job never passes an earlier job of its own target, and moving marks every waiting job `moved` so the owner's order beats grouping; `waiting` is the keys in order. Jobs sent to ComfyUI can't be moved. **The queue page** `/queue` (`QueuePage.tsx`, linked as “Open queue” at the bottom of the header list and as “Queue” in the site menu): Pause/Resume, Working, Waiting (Move up, Move down, Do next; jobs still gathering clicks are listed last without them), Finished, Failed, and History (`GET /api/picture-jobs?recent=1&limit=&before=`, every story, temporary jobs left out, “Show more”). Test: `tests/e2e/queue-page.e2e.mjs`. **Writing joins the queue (task 1.4):** items are now `PictureItem | RunItem` (`RunItem`: an AI run, `type: "run"`, kept apart from the picture list, which `usePictureQueue()` still returns alone). `startRun(runId)`, `stopRun`, `resumeRun` and `listenRun` are on `useWorkQueue()`; a run is claimed with this tab's token (`TAB_TOKEN`, new at each page load), set `queued` on the server, and the queue gives it one `runNextStep` (`src/lib/aiRuns/runner.ts`) at a time, then puts it back in line. On opening the app (and coming back into view) `GET /api/ai-runs?unfinished=1` adds the runs this tab doesn't have: held by another tab as “elsewhere” (Open only), the others as paused with Resume; nothing starts by itself. The decision is `schedule()` in `schedule.ts` (`nextActions` is its actions): with `settings.queue.sharing` “together” writing and pictures go at once; with “turns” (the default) the machine belongs to one kind at a time (pictures while any job is in ComfyUI, writing while a step runs), the holder keeps it until `switchMinutes` / `switchItems` are used up and the other kind has waiting work, and the provider keeps the `holder` it returns. Held picture jobs get the progress stage `held` (“Waiting its turn”); the header list's first line (`machineNote`) says who has the machine; `ended` and the end of a step call `pump` so the other kind takes over. Closing the tab while writing goes on or pictures are held asks first (`beforeunload`). For the browser tests the provider sets `window.__storytimeQueue` when a page has set `window.__STORYTIME_TEST__` (the run page that starts runs comes in task 1.5). Test: `tests/e2e/work-queue.e2e.mjs`. **The run page** is `/c/<id>/write-ahead` (`WriteAhead.tsx`: the Fill form, the estimate, an unfinished run offered first; `AiRunProgress.tsx`: a run in progress, shared with the picture runs of milestone 4; `?run=<id>` shows a run). It follows the queue's item for the run (`listenRun`) and polls the server's copy otherwise; Try again / Skip use `retryTask` / `skipTask`; the plan check (D23) edits go to `POST /api/ai-runs/<id>/review`. While a run is writing or waiting the provider asks for a screen wake lock. Test: `tests/e2e/write-ahead.e2e.mjs`. **Generate from the writing page** is a queue item (`GenerateItem`, key `gen:<draft key>`; `generate(request)`, `stopGenerate`, `listenDraft` on `useWorkQueue()`): `ChapterEditor` hands it `GenerateRequest` (the story context, direction, models, what to do after, the draft's text when it started, the draft itself) and its busy state follows the item, so it goes on when the author leaves the page. It is a writing step for `schedule()` (so with turns on it can wait for pictures, and the page says “Waiting for pictures to finish (2 left)”), and goes before waiting runs (D16). While a writing page for the draft is listening it applies the text to its own state (`applyGenerated` with `isBegun`, in `src/lib/generatedDraft.ts`) and its autosave saves it; with no page listening the queue saves the draft itself every 3 s and at the end (`getDraft` + `saveDraft` with its `updatedAt`, once more after a conflict, then an error on the item with Copy text), and afterwards the choice text and suggested choices go in (`applyAfterGenerate`). Picture jobs are now sent one after another (each once the one before is queued on ComfyUI) so ComfyUI gets them in the order the queue chose. Test: `tests/e2e/work-queue.e2e.mjs`. It holds every job for the whole app, so a job goes on, and is recorded, when the author moves to another page in the app. `ComfyGenerate` builds the job's input and calls its `ask()`; the gathering of quick clicks (off when `settings.comfy.batchClicks` is off, the Settings › Drawing checkbox: each click is then its own job), the upload, queueing (`queueJob` in `src/lib/comfy/makeJob.ts`), waiting, Stop and recording happen there, and `ComfyGenerate` shows the jobs of its own chapter. A chapter's page `listen`s for its chapter (the cover page for its story's cover; listeners are kept by `targetKey`): a job that ends while the page is open is handed to it at once (pictures, or the error as the status line); one that ends while it isn't is kept (`state: "done"` or `"failed"`) and handed over when the page opens, and the workspace marks its pictures fresh even if the page already loaded them from the log. A job still gathering clicks when the page is left is sent as usual. On opening the app, and when it comes back into view (at most every 10 s), the queue asks ComfyUI (`listTaggedJobs(url)`, every chapter) for StoryTime's jobs: those being made are shown again, and finished ones the log doesn't have (`POST /api/picture-jobs/known {prompt_ids}`) are recorded and kept as new. **New until seen** (`picture_jobs.seen_at`, migration 19; older jobs count as seen): the queue records a job with `unseen: true` when its chapter's page isn't open, and such jobs are listed by `GET /api/picture-jobs?unseen=1` (every story, latest 50), which the queue loads on opening and when the app comes back into view, so a job that finished elsewhere (another device, or before a reload) shows as ready in the header. The Illustrate page shows a chapter's unseen jobs as fresh (`picturesOf`) and marks them seen (`POST /api/picture-jobs/seen {ids}`, `markJobsSeen`); the queue does the same when it hands a job to an open page or one is dismissed. Those pictures (unseen as the page loaded, or handed over by the queue when the page opens) also get `isNew`: a green “✦ New” badge in the tile's top left corner (`.picture-new`, in `InUseBadge`), taken off a picture when it or one of its buttons is clicked (`onTouched`, a click-capture on the tile, so a scroll doesn't count), when it is shown in the viewer, or when it changes (any `onChange`). It isn't kept anywhere, so leaving the page takes every badge off. Pictures that finish while the page is open get none. **In the header** (`WorkQueueStatus.tsx`, beside ☰, hidden when the queue is empty) a button shows the jobs being made (a spinner and their number) and those kept (“N ready”, and ⚠ for failed ones); it opens a list (`role=region` “Queue”), newest first, of each job's chapter and story (from `GET /api/chapters/labels?ids=`; a cover job reads “Cover” with its story, from `&stories=`), moment, progress and Stop, or its thumbnails with Open (the chapter's Illustrate page, which takes them as new pictures) and Dismiss, and Clear finished. The tab's title gets the number of jobs being made after it: “StoryTime (2)”. Tests: `tests/e2e/picture-queue.e2e.mjs`, and `picture-queue-shared.e2e.mjs` for a ComfyUI shared with others (the fake's `addJob` puts a job somebody else made in it).
- **Make missing pictures** (`/s/<id>/pictures`, `MissingPictures.tsx`; from the outline's menu, the chapter's ☰ “Make missing pictures…” (`?from=<chapter>`), and a finished write-ahead run's “Make pictures for these chapters” (`?run=<id>`)) is a run of mode `pictures` (`planPictures` in `src/lib/aiRuns/plan.ts`: chapters in outline order, a header before its footer, slots that already have a picture left out; `PictureTask` is `todo`, then `written` (prompt written), then `done` (saved)). It starts with Header pictures, off for reference pictures (they are used only when the form's box is ticked *and* the drawing preset supports them) and the cheap model for prompts. The runner (`pickStep`/`doPrompt` in `runner.ts`, helpers in `src/lib/aiRuns/pictures.ts`) only writes prompts, **all of them before any picture is drawn** (so the text model and the picture model are each loaded once), saving each as the chapter's prompt boxes (`PUT .../picture-draft`) so the Illustrate page shows it; when no prompt is left to write but pictures are still being drawn it answers `idle`, and the item stays “Drawing pictures”. **Drawing goes through the picture queue, not a writing slot**: `pictureTick` in `WorkQueue.tsx` keeps the run in line for writing while a prompt is left, then asks the queue for each written task (`buildPictureJob`: preset and always-include text, LoRAs, size, family; a session job `pictures:<run id>` with `base` the task id, so it is never “ready” in the header), `pictureMade` saves the result with `savePicture` (`PUT /api/chapters/:id/images/:slot?job=&index=0`, which also pins it) and `flushPictures` writes the results into the run between steps. A failed job is asked again once, then the run fails with the error. Stop and Resume work as for writing runs. **A task whose job finished while the page was closed may make a second picture on Resume** (the plan accepts it). The run page (`?picrun=<id>`, which the header list and the queue page link to through `runHref`) is `AiRunProgress` with picture rows (thumbnail, Try again, Skip). Tests: `tests/unit/ai-runs-pictures.test.mts`, `ai-runs-plan`, `ai-runs-runner`, `tests/e2e/missing-pictures.e2e.mjs`. Not tried against a real model or ComfyUI.
- **ComfyUI code** (`src/lib/comfy/`): `client.ts` (HTTP and WebSocket calls, `uploadImage`), `workflow.ts` (filling a workflow; see below), `startPicture.ts` (`StartPicture`, `fromComfyImage`, `fromSaved`, `uploadStart`), `presets.ts` (the named presets, below).
- **Workflows.** Built-in SDXL text-to-image, and `BUILTIN_IMG2IMG` for a redraw from a picture (the Refine screen's jobs; the Illustrate page's starting picture is only for Instruct edits, which use an edit model's workflow). A drawing preset of a **model family** (`family` other than `checkpoint`: Z-Image, Flux, Chroma, Qwen-Image, Krea 2; `src/lib/comfy/drawModels.ts`) has its own built-in workflow instead, built by `buildDraw` from the files `familyPresetCheck` found (`queueJob` does it, not `fillWorkflow`): UNETLoader (node "1"), CLIPLoader (text encoder, "2"; Flux's is a DualCLIPLoader with CLIP-L and T5-XXL), VAELoader ("3"), the family's own nodes (Z-Image: ModelSamplingAuraFlow shift 3, the negative a ConditioningZeroOut of the positive; Flux: FluxGuidance and a ConditioningZeroOut negative; Chroma: a real negative CLIPTextEncode at CFG 4, behind `T5TokenizerOptions` only when `/object_info` lists that node, an optional node the check records in `FamilyCheck.optionalNodes`; Qwen-Image: ModelSamplingAuraFlow shift 3.1 and a real negative at CFG 2.5, its model part excluding "edit" so it never takes Qwen-Image-Edit's files, as the edit model's name needs "edit"; Krea 2 (Kroma): a `krea2` CLIPLoader with Qwen 3 VL 4B, the Wan 2.1 VAE, 8 steps of euler at CFG 1 and a ConditioningZeroOut negative, then, for text to image only, a refine pass (nodes 40-43, after the LoRA ids 30+: ImageScaleBy lanczos by the `upscale` setting, default 2, set it to 1 to skip; VAEEncode; a second KSampler of 4 steps res_multistep beta at denoise 0.14; VAEDecode, which SaveImage then saves), so pictures come out `upscale` times the chosen size; `upscale` is a `DrawTuning` knob next to shift and guidance), `LoraLoaderModelOnly` LoRAs after the model loader, `EmptySD3LatentImage` for the latent (a redraw from a picture: LoadImage → ImageScale to the size, in multiples of 16 → VAEEncode → RepeatLatentBatch, with the sampler's `denoise`), KSampler ("8"), VAEDecode and SaveImage. A part whose file is `.gguf` is switched to the ComfyUI-GGUF loader (`switchToGgufLoaders`, shared with the edit models in `modelParts.ts`). `usesFamily(preset)` (a family and no pasted workflow) decides: a pasted workflow overrides the family, as it overrides everything. A custom workflow (pasted in a drawing preset's card in Settings) is filled by placeholders (`{{prompt}} {{negative}} {{checkpoint}} {{width}} {{height}} {{count}} {{seed}} {{prefix}}`, and for image to image `{{image}} {{denoise}}`), or, with none, by finding the usual nodes. A custom workflow with no placeholders is started from a picture by swapping its `EmptyLatentImage` for LoadImage → VAEEncode → RepeatLatentBatch and setting every numeric `denoise` input; it needs a VAE (a `VAEDecode`'s link or a `CheckpointLoaderSimple`). `describeWorkflow(...).image` says whether a workflow can. To redraw only a painted area, `PictureParams.mask` is the name of a mask PNG in ComfyUI's input folder (same size as the picture, white = redraw): the built-in `BUILTIN_INPAINT` and a placeholder-less workflow add LoadImageMask → SetLatentNoiseMask before the sampler and paste the new pictures over the original outside the mask (InvertMask, ImageCompositeMasked) before saving; a placeholder workflow needs `{{mask}}` in its own LoadImageMask, or filling it throws. `describeWorkflow(...).mask` says whether a workflow can. **Reference pictures** (`src/lib/comfy/references.ts`: `Reference` = `StoredReference` plus `key`, `thumb` and `blob`, made from a `StartPicture` by `asReference`; at most 4; default weights character 0.4, item 0.4, place 0.3, style 0.8; character, item and place references stop at half the steps, so the model's own style comes back, a style reference runs to the end): `JobInput.refs` and `JobInput.ipAdapter` (the preset's base's `IpAdapterCheck`, from `drawPresetIpAdapter(editModels, draw)`, as `modelChecksFor` finds it with the other checks). `queueJob` uploads each reference once per key and ComfyUI address (`storytime-ref-….png`, `uploadReference`), then `fillWorkflow` (`PictureParams.refs` and `.ipAdapter`) calls `withReferences` (`ipAdapter.ts`) on the filled workflow, after the LoRAs: an `IPAdapterModelLoader` (`ref-loader`) and `CLIPVisionLoader` (`ref-clip-vision`), and per reference a `LoadImage` (`ref-N-image`) and an `IPAdapterAdvanced` (`ref-N`; weight type linear, “style transfer” for a style; concat, start 0, end 0.8, or 1 for a style; embeds scaling V only) chained from the sampler's model, which every `KSampler`/`KSamplerAdvanced` then takes from the end of the chain. This works for the built-in workflows and pasted ones without placeholders (`describeWorkflow(...).refs`); a placeholder workflow or a model family preset says it can't, and a job with references fails with a message naming what IP-Adapter is missing (`ipAdapterProblem`) rather than going without. An edit takes up to two references (Kontext one) as `EditParams.refs`: Qwen-Image-Edit's two `TextEncodeQwenImageEditPlus` nodes get them as `image2`/`image3`, Kontext a second chained `ReferenceLatent` (LoadImage → ImageScale → VAEEncode); an edit needs no IP-Adapter. With references, `framedForRefs` (`editModels.ts`) puts a sentence before the instruction saying image 1 (Kontext: the first picture) is the one edited and the others only show what to take from; without it the models blend the starting picture into the reference. `sameJob` treats different references (key, role, weight) as a different job. `ComfyGenerate` takes `refs`/`onRefs`/`refChoices` props from the prompt panel (see **The References section**). A model family's workflow takes the mask the same way (`buildDraw` calls `redrawOnlyMask`, pasting back the scaled picture, node "11"). A redraw below full strength with the built-in workflow or a model family runs only the steps its strength needs: `partialSteps` (`workflow.ts`) turns the sampler (node "3", or "8" in a family) into a `KSamplerAdvanced` that starts at `steps - stepsToRun(steps, denoise)` (the share of the steps, at least 4), keeping its id and links; custom workflows keep their own sampler, and edit models are left alone.

A job whose record is refused with 404 (its chapter was deleted meanwhile) is dropped without a warning. A database copied from another StoryTime has the same chapter ids, so older jobs (without `instance`) for them can't be told apart; new ones can. A temporary job carries `temporary: true` in its `JobRecord` (from `JobInput.temporary`, which `sameJob` includes so a temporary click never joins a normal job), so a job found again after a reload is recorded as temporary; `WorkQueue` records it as seen, hands its pictures (`Picture.temporary`) to an open page for its target, and when no page is open drops it instead of keeping it as “ready”. A Refine job also carries `session` (and `base`) in the tag (not in `JobRecord`), and the queue gives it only to that session's Refine screen (`listenSession`, see the Refine bullet), never to the target's page. Saved pictures are filed under `StoryTime/<story id>/<chapter id>` where the workflow's prefix is `StoryTime` or contains `{{prefix}}`.
- **Keep seed** (`ComfyGenerate`, under the settings, above Queue): ticked, every queue uses the seed in the box (filled with a random one, or typed by hand, a whole number; New seed re-rolls it) instead of a new one, and it goes into `JobInput.seed` ahead of Reuse seed. Queues of the same prompt and seed still join one job, and the pictures of one batch differ from each other (ComfyUI makes each batch item's noise from the one seed), so the batch size isn't limited; only a separate later job with the same everything repeats earlier pictures.
- **Export workflow** buttons on each drawing and edit preset card (`exportWorkflow.ts`) download the API-format workflow StoryTime would send, with a sample prompt or instruction (an edit loads `example.png` from ComfyUI's input folder), for trying in ComfyUI by hand. A model-family or edit preset needs Check setup to have found its files first.
- **Settings** (`settings.comfy`, in `src/lib/settingsSchema.ts`): url, width, height, `coverWidth` and `coverHeight` (the cover page's size), `drawPresets`, `editPresets` and `editPreset` (the edit preset last used). A drawing preset holds a `family` ("checkpoint", the default and what presets saved before families parse as, or a model family), a checkpoint (the "checkpoint" family's, and what `{{checkpoint}}` is in a pasted workflow), `files` (by part key, "" or missing = found by name) and `textEncoderDevice` for the other families, LoRAs, Redraw strength (where the Refine screen's Strength starts; the Illustrate page has no slider for it), sampler settings (`Partial<DrawTuning>`: steps, CFG, sampler, scheduler and, for the families that have them, `shift` and `guidance`) and an optional workflow. A "checkpoint" preset also has an optional `base` ("sdxl", "illustrious", "noobai" or "pony"; missing = `guessBase` of the checkpoint's file name, `src/lib/comfy/ipAdapter.ts`), set by the Model base select in Settings; it picks the IP-Adapter files for reference pictures, and the setup check reports them per preset as `DrawCheck.ipAdapter` (informational only: a preset without IP-Adapter is fully usable; jobs that carry references need it, see Workflows). Choosing a family in Settings sets the preset's `promptStyle` to sentences and clears its `sampler` overrides; the Advanced settings start from the family's defaults (`DrawModel.defaults`). The setup check reports a family preset per preset (`DrawCheck.family`, a `FamilyCheck`: parts, missing nodes and options, `ggufNeeded`), and the Illustrate page gets the same checks as `EditModels.families` (`drawPresetFamily`), which `ComfyGenerate` hands to `queueJob` as `JobInput.family` (null for a checkpoint preset or one with a workflow of its own); the job log's checkpoint column holds the family's model file (`madeWithText`). An edit preset holds one edit model's files, LoRAs, Fast box (set in Settings only; the Illustrate page has no Fast box, and an edit is fast when the preset says so and ComfyUI has the speed-up file), `textEncoderDevice` ("default" or "cpu", the text encoder loader's `device`), default strength and sampler settings (`src/lib/comfy/presets.ts`). **Edit models and GGUF** (`src/lib/comfy/editModels.ts`): a model part's file can be a `.gguf` (needs ComfyUI-GGUF's `UnetLoaderGGUF`, `CLIPLoaderGGUF` or `DualCLIPLoaderGGUF`, a part's `gguf`). A part's choices are its normal loader's list plus the GGUF loader's, and the GGUF nodes are needed (for `ready`) only when a `.gguf` file is in use; the check carries what `/object_info` says about each loader (`EditCheck.loaders`, `ggufMissing`, `devices`). `buildEdit` switches the loader node of a part whose file is `.gguf` to the GGUF loader and keeps only the inputs that loader lists (so `weight_dtype` goes), and drops `device` when the text encoder loader in use doesn't list it or the value; LoRA chains are unchanged. A safetensors file is preferred to a GGUF one when both match. Settings marks `.gguf` choices "(GGUF)" and says to install ComfyUI-GGUF when its nodes are absent. Test: `tests/e2e/illustrate-gguf.e2e.mjs` (the fake ComfyUI's `gguf: true`); the drawing families': `tests/unit/draw-models.test.mts` and `tests/e2e/illustrate-families.e2e.mjs`. They are shared across stories and held on the server. (`count` and the top-level `imagePromptStyle` are left over and unused.)
- **The story's picture settings** (`StoryPictureSettings` on the story's details page, `#pictures`): art style (`stories.art_style`) and drawing preset (`stories.draw_preset`, migration 18; null follows the first preset). A chapter can choose another preset (`chapters.draw_preset`, null follows the story's), from the Model Settings panel (next to its Preset menu is a gear link to the preset in Settings), whose Drawing preset menu marks the story's preset "(default)". They keep a story's pictures alike, so they are the story's, not the chapter's or the app's. `stories.checkpoint` (migration 16) is only read once, to turn the checkpoints stories used into presets. The chapter's Illustrate page does not show them (the art style is added to prompts, and the preset is in Model Settings); the cover prompt panel still shows them as one small muted line (`StoryPicturesLine`). **The prompt style** is no longer the story's: it is `promptStyle` on each drawing preset (Settings › Drawing), and prompts are written in the style of the chapter's preset, else the story's. There are three (`STYLE_RULES` in `src/lib/ai/imagePrompt.ts`, shared by chapter, cover and codex prompts): `tags` (tags and short phrases, for SDXL and SD 1.5 checkpoints), `booru` (Danbooru and e621 tags only, in a fixed order: count, framing, traits, action, setting; for Illustrious, NoobAI and Pony) and `natural` (sentences, for Flux and SD3). Both tag styles share rules against repeating a trait in other words, mood words and quality tags, and say to give secondary characters only the traits that tell them apart; `isTagStyle` makes `booru` join, dedupe and take the art style like `tags`. Chapter prompts are also told to pick the framing for the moment (close-up to wide shot) rather than full body by default; codex prompts keep their full-body picture. `stories.image_prompt_style` is left over and unused. The prompt panel's write button is an icon (sparkle, then a redo arrow once a prompt was written) beside the eye button; its accessible names stay "Write prompt" / "Write again". The page title is `Illustrate “<chapter title>”`; the `IllustrateChapter` panel is headed "Chapter Text" (no title line), with its Summary and the whole chapter and Story so far each in a fold that starts closed and is remembered in the browser. While a prompt is being written the write button becomes a Stop button (same size, `StopIcon`). The Pictures panel has no visible title: its view buttons are on the left of one line, and the order and Clear unpinned buttons are on the right. They are in the JSON export and import. **The story's style picture** (`stories.style_ref`, migration 26: a JSON `StoredReference` with role "style", null for none; `PATCH /api/stories/:id` takes `style_ref`, role must be style and weight 0 to 1; not in the JSON export, since the pictures it names are tied to ids that change on import): the “Style picture (optional)” part of the `#pictures` card has a thumbnail, Choose… (the `ReferencePicker` from `References.tsx` with `noUpload`: Suggested, Pictures and Codex from the cover's pool, since an upload can't be kept), a Strength slider (0.8 to start with) and Remove, saved with Save picture settings. The Illustrate, cover and codex pages get it as `story.style_ref` and hand `ComfyGenerate` a `styleRef` (`restoreReference`; a picture that is gone, or not in the page's pool, is left out): the References section shows it first as a fixed “Story style” row (role Style, its strength, set on the details page) with a **Use the story's style picture** box, ticked by default, and kept in this browser per page and moment (`localStorage`, `storytime:style-ref-off:<target>:<moment>`, not on the server). While ticked it counts toward the 4 references and goes first in the job's references (weight type “style transfer”), and is in the job log like any reference; it is not shown or sent on the Instruct tab, and a reference of the same picture already in the list stops it being sent twice. Test: `tests/e2e/story-style-ref.e2e.mjs`.
