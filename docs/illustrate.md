# How chapter pictures work

Notes for whoever changes the Illustrate page (`src/app/c/[id]/illustrate/page.tsx`) or the cover page (`src/app/s/[id]/cover/page.tsx`), and the file to read before touching them. All ComfyUI calls are made from the browser, never from the StoryTime server. Planned work is in `TODO.md`; how to set up ComfyUI is in `docs/pictures.md`.

- **Targets.** Pictures are made for a target (`PictureTarget` in `src/lib/pictureTarget.ts`), a union with a `kind`: `chapter`, `cover` (the story's) or `codex` (an entry, `entryId`). The queue, job tags, file prefix, browser storage (`stateKey`), saver and Pictures panel all take one, and ask it rather than checking its kind: `slotsFor` (the chapter's header and footer, `cover`, or `codex`), `targetKey` (`chapter:<id>`, `cover:<story id>`, `codex:<entry id>`), `targetHref`, `presetApi`, `othersQuery`, `savedPictureApi`. The stored formats carry a chapter id that is null for cover and codex jobs (`chapterIdOf`, `targetOfIds`).
- **Codex pictures** (migration 25; `src/lib/codexPictures.ts`). An entry's picture can change over the story, on its branch only, like codex text: `codex_pictures` rows are anchored at a chapter (null = the whole story), apply to it and its descendants, and `codexPictureAt` / `codexPicturesAt` pick the deepest anchor on the root-to-chapter path, then the latest (`posted_at`); removing one only hides it (`removed_at`). APIs: `GET /api/stories/:id/codex-pictures?chapter=` (each entry's picture there, and `ids`), `&entry=` (all of an entry's pictures and the current one), `POST ?entry=&anchor=&job=&index=` (image body; saves and pins the job's picture), `GET/DELETE .../codex-pictures/:pictureId`, `GET/PUT /codex-draft?entry=` (`codex_picture_prompts`, with `refs`) and `/codex-preset?entry=`. A codex job is in `picture_jobs` with `entry_id` and the moment `codex` (no chapter); the cover's job lists leave them out, and `GET /api/picture-jobs?story=&entry=` lists an entry's. Its ComfyUI tag is `{chapterId: null, entryId, moment: "codex"}` (old tags without `entryId` still read as cover jobs) and its files go under `StoryTime/<story id>/codex/<entry id>`. The page `/s/<id>/codex/<entry>` (`?at=<chapter id>`: the chapter the author came from, whose text and picture it shows) is `CodexWorkspace`: `CodexPromptPanel` (the prompt is written from the entry's text as of that chapter, `src/lib/ai/codexPrompt.ts`: one full-body picture of a character, the object, or an establishing picture of a place, on a plain background, sharing the tag and sentence rules and `PromptFields`) and `CodexPictureCard` as the destination. A picture's ☰ menu offers Use as codex picture, from the start, and (with `?at=`) from “<chapter>” on (`SaveAnchor` in `usePictureSaver`). The Codex panel (`CodexPanel`, given `storyId`) shows each entry's picture as of its chapter as a thumbnail and a Make a picture / Picture link to the page with `?at=`; the picture queue's list names the job “<entry name> (codex)” (`GET /api/chapters/labels?entries=`). Tests: `tests/e2e/codex-pictures.e2e.mjs`, `tests/api/codex-pictures.test.mjs`, `tests/unit/codex-prompt.test.mts`.
- **Codex references.** The References picker has a **Codex** tab: the entries with a picture as of the page's chapter (`RefPool.codex`, from `codexRefChoices`; on the cover page the whole-story pictures), added as Character, Item or Place by the entry's type with the entry's name as label and source `codex:<picture id>`. Suggested also lists an entry whose name or alias is in the prompt or What to show (whole word, any case; `mentions`). A kept `codex:` reference is restored while its picture exists (`RefPool.codexIds`).
- **The cover page** (`/s/<id>/cover`, linked as Make a cover from the Cover image card on the story's details page) is the Illustrate page for the story's cover: `CoverWorkspace` lays out the same columns with `CoverPromptPanel` (prompt, then `ComfyGenerate`) and, in the gallery, a `ChapterImageEditor` for the `cover` slot and `ChapterPictures` (its last view is Chapters: every chapter's pictures, `?story=&chapters=1`). The ☰ menu offers Use as cover. **Write from** says what the prompt is written from (`coverPromptRequest` in `src/lib/ai/coverPrompt.ts`, which shares the tags and sentence rules with chapter prompts): the summary and codex, the first chapter, or a path to a chosen chapter (the Path ends at menu lists the chapters in the outline's order; the path and its codex are loaded with `GET /api/chapters/:id` and `/codex`). A story with no chapters can only use the summary. The boxes, the source and the chosen chapter are kept in `cover_prompts` (migration 20, with the References as `refs` from migration 24; `src/lib/coverPictures.ts`; `GET/PUT /api/stories/:id/cover-draft`, saved by `useCoverDraft`), and so is the cover's own drawing preset (`/cover-preset`; null follows the story's). Cover pictures have their own size (`settings.comfy.coverWidth/coverHeight`, 832 × 1216 by default, with a Size menu of tall sizes first), so choosing one doesn't change the chapters'. The saved cover can be a starting picture (`saved:<story id>:cover`). Uploading a cover still works on the details page.
- **Components** (`src/components/`): `usePictureWorkspace` holds the state a picture page shares (the prompt loaded from a picture, the starting picture, the jobs being made, the column width, and all of the target's pictures as `Picture`s from `pictures.ts`: the page's jobs, then those made on this visit, marked `fresh`). `IllustrateWorkspace` uses it and lays out the two columns: `.illustrate-controls` (the `IllustrateChapter` panel, passed in by the page, then `ImagePromptPanel`: prompt, then `ComfyGenerate`) and `.illustrate-gallery` (the two `ChapterImageEditor` cards first, to compare new pictures with, then `ChapterPictures`). In one column (narrower than 64rem) the two `ChapterImageEditor` cards come first, above the chapter panel (CSS: the gallery is `display: contents` and the cards have `order: -1`). On a wide screen (64rem) the controls column is sticky and the page uses up to 120rem. The Size menu shows each option as columns: shape glyph, name, aspect ratio, pixels (`SizeColumns`, laid out by `.size-select` with the browser's customizable select, `appearance: base-select`, which needs an explicit `<button><selectedcontent>` child so the closed menu has the columns too; browsers without it show the same text in an ordinary menu). The story's art style is shown in small type under the Prompt box (`.prompt-art-style`), since it is added to every prompt. `ComfyGenerate` makes one picture per click by default; the Pictures per click menu beside Queue (`settings.comfy.queueCount`: 1, 2, 4, 5 or 8, on the server) makes one click call `ask()` that many times, so they gather into one job like quick clicks (there is no How many menu; `settings.comfy.count` is left over and unused): clicks with nothing changed, each within 0.8 s of the last (`GATHER_MS`), are gathered into one job of up to 8 pictures (`MAX_BATCH`), and a click with anything changed sends the gathered job at once and starts another. Several jobs can wait in ComfyUI's queue, each with the prompt and settings as they were when asked for. It hands the jobs being made up through `onQueue` (as `PendingJob`s, newest first, each with its `stop`) and each finished job's pictures through `onPictures`; `ChapterPictures` shows each job as a place kept for each of its pictures (`li.comfy-pending-tile` in the picture grid, at the size setting's shape, or the starting picture's once it is uploaded), with the job's progress drawn over each place (only the first is a `role=status`, and has Stop). Stop only interrupts a job ComfyUI's `/queue` says is running, since older ComfyUIs interrupt whatever is running.
- **The Pictures panel** (`ChapterPictures`) is a timeline, newest first by default: the jobs being made (newest first, so the one running is last, next to the newest pictures), then the pictures. The button beside Clear unpinned switches it to oldest first (`settings.comfy.picturesOldestFirst`, kept on the server so it is the same on every device): the runs, and the pictures inside each, are reversed and a job being made comes after its pictures, so the newest is at the bottom; the viewer follows the order shown. It does not apply to Other chapters, which is always newest first. `timeline()` in `pictures.ts` puts neighbours made with the same prompt, negative prompt, checkpoint, moment, starting picture, strength and whether a painted area was used (not which: a job being made doesn't know its mask's file name yet) (and size, from the prompt alone) in one group, so two batches in a row share a group and A, B, A is three groups; a job being made joins the newest group when its settings match. Groups have no heading, only a line between them (`.picture-group`'s top border); each group's list is named `Pictures from “<prompt>”` for screen readers (and the tests). Other chapters likewise shows only the chapter titles, with a line between jobs. Jobs being made join the groups in Pinned & new and All; in Rejected and Other chapters they show on their own at the top. Views (`inView` in `pictures.ts`): **Pinned & new** (default: pinned, plus this visit's unmarked ones, and those of jobs that finished while the page wasn't open, until Clear unpinned), **All** (everything not rejected), **Rejected**, and **Other chapters** (`OtherChapterPictures`: the story's other chapters, paged, through `?story=&not_chapter=`). So on return only pinned pictures show. Clear keeps nothing on the server. Each picture has a row of round buttons on its top right corner (`PictureButtons`, shared by `PictureTile` and the full-size viewer, so they look and sit the same in both): pin (a thumbtack, filled when pinned) and reject (✕, ↺ to restore), and a ☰ menu with Use as header picture and Use as footer picture (icons from `PictureIcons.tsx`; the slot it is in reads "This is the header picture" and is marked unavailable), Redraw from this, Change part of this…, Edit this, Load prompt, Info and Open in a new tab. A picture made from a painted area has “painted area” in its caption. **Info** (`PictureInfo`, a modal `<dialog>`, also over the viewer and for Other chapters' pictures) shows the prompt, negative prompt and `pictureDetails()` (chapter and moment, when made, checkpoint, size, seed, batch size and index, starting picture and strength, “Area: painted part only” when a mask was used, file), with Copy prompt, Copy negative prompt, Copy all as Markdown (`pictureMarkdown()`, prompts in code blocks), Load prompt and Start from this. Copying works over plain http too (`copyText` falls back to a hidden text box, put inside the dialog because the page behind a modal can't be selected). The workflow's other settings (sampler, steps, CFG) aren't recorded, so Info can't show them. Choosing an item puts the keyboard back on ☰. A mark change saves at once, is put back if the server refuses, and offers Undo. The picture that is the chapter's header or footer now has a badge on its top left corner (here and in the viewer): `chapter_images.image_index` (migration 15) says which of the job's pictures was saved, sent as `PUT .../images/:slot?job=<id>&index=<n>`; one saved before that is only recognised when its job made a single picture. Clicking a picture opens `PictureViewer` (a modal `<dialog>`, whole screen) on it, going through the view's pictures in the order shown: ← → or a swipe, P pins, X rejects (a picture that leaves the view moves the viewer on), Esc closes (or closes the ☰ menu when it is open). In the viewer the picture sits in a frame of its own shape (`.viewer-frame`, sized with container units), so its buttons and badge are on its corners as on a tile. Ctrl/Cmd/Shift or middle click still opens the picture in a new tab. `usePictureSaver` saves a picture as the header or footer.
- **The picture queue** (`PictureQueue.tsx`, `PictureQueueProvider` in the root layout, `usePictureQueue()`) holds every job for the whole app, so a job goes on, and is recorded, when the author moves to another page in the app. `ComfyGenerate` builds the job's input and calls its `ask()`; the gathering of quick clicks, the upload, queueing (`queueJob` in `src/lib/comfy/makeJob.ts`), waiting, Stop and recording happen there, and `ComfyGenerate` shows the jobs of its own chapter. A chapter's page `listen`s for its chapter (the cover page for its story's cover; listeners are kept by `targetKey`): a job that ends while the page is open is handed to it at once (pictures, or the error as the status line); one that ends while it isn't is kept (`state: "done"` or `"failed"`) and handed over when the page opens, and the workspace marks its pictures fresh even if the page already loaded them from the log. A job still gathering clicks when the page is left is sent as usual. On opening the app, and when it comes back into view (at most every 10 s), the queue asks ComfyUI (`listTaggedJobs(url)`, every chapter) for StoryTime's jobs: those being made are shown again, and finished ones the log doesn't have (`POST /api/picture-jobs/known {prompt_ids}`) are recorded and kept as new. **New until seen** (`picture_jobs.seen_at`, migration 19; older jobs count as seen): the queue records a job with `unseen: true` when its chapter's page isn't open, and such jobs are listed by `GET /api/picture-jobs?unseen=1` (every story, latest 50), which the queue loads on opening and when the app comes back into view, so a job that finished elsewhere (another device, or before a reload) shows as ready in the header. The Illustrate page shows a chapter's unseen jobs as fresh (`picturesOf`) and marks them seen (`POST /api/picture-jobs/seen {ids}`, `markJobsSeen`); the queue does the same when it hands a job to an open page or one is dismissed. Those pictures (unseen as the page loaded, or handed over by the queue when the page opens) also get `isNew`: a green “✦ New” badge in the tile's top left corner (`.picture-new`, in `InUseBadge`), taken off a picture when it or one of its buttons is clicked (`onTouched`, a click-capture on the tile, so a scroll doesn't count), when it is shown in the viewer, or when it changes (any `onChange`). It isn't kept anywhere, so leaving the page takes every badge off. Pictures that finish while the page is open get none. **In the header** (`PictureQueueStatus`, beside ☰, hidden when the queue is empty) a button shows the jobs being made (a spinner and their number) and those kept (“N ready”, and ⚠ for failed ones); it opens a list (`role=region` “Picture jobs”), newest first, of each job's chapter and story (from `GET /api/chapters/labels?ids=`; a cover job reads “Cover” with its story, from `&stories=`), moment, progress and Stop, or its thumbnails with Open (the chapter's Illustrate page, which takes them as new pictures) and Dismiss, and Clear finished. The tab's title gets the number of jobs being made after it: “StoryTime (2)”. Tests: `tests/e2e/picture-queue.e2e.mjs`, and `picture-queue-shared.e2e.mjs` for a ComfyUI shared with others (the fake's `addJob` puts a job somebody else made in it).
- **ComfyUI code** (`src/lib/comfy/`): `client.ts` (HTTP and WebSocket calls, `uploadImage`), `workflow.ts` (filling a workflow; see below), `startPicture.ts` (`StartPicture`, `fromComfyImage`, `fromSaved`, `uploadStart`), `presets.ts` (the named presets, below).
- **Workflows.** Built-in SDXL text-to-image, and `BUILTIN_IMG2IMG` when a starting picture is chosen. A drawing preset of a **model family** (`family` other than `checkpoint`: Z-Image, Flux, Chroma, Qwen-Image; `src/lib/comfy/drawModels.ts`) has its own built-in workflow instead, built by `buildDraw` from the files `familyPresetCheck` found (`queueJob` does it, not `fillWorkflow`): UNETLoader (node "1"), CLIPLoader (text encoder, "2"; Flux's is a DualCLIPLoader with CLIP-L and T5-XXL), VAELoader ("3"), the family's own nodes (Z-Image: ModelSamplingAuraFlow shift 3, the negative a ConditioningZeroOut of the positive; Flux: FluxGuidance and a ConditioningZeroOut negative; Chroma: a real negative CLIPTextEncode at CFG 4, behind `T5TokenizerOptions` only when `/object_info` lists that node, an optional node the check records in `FamilyCheck.optionalNodes`; Qwen-Image: ModelSamplingAuraFlow shift 3.1 and a real negative at CFG 2.5, its model part excluding "edit" so it never takes Qwen-Image-Edit's files, as the edit model's name needs "edit"), `LoraLoaderModelOnly` LoRAs after the model loader, `EmptySD3LatentImage` for the latent (Redraw: LoadImage → ImageScale to the size, in multiples of 16 → VAEEncode → RepeatLatentBatch, with the sampler's `denoise`), KSampler ("8"), VAEDecode and SaveImage. A part whose file is `.gguf` is switched to the ComfyUI-GGUF loader (`switchToGgufLoaders`, shared with the edit models in `modelParts.ts`). `usesFamily(preset)` (a family and no pasted workflow) decides: a pasted workflow overrides the family, as it overrides everything. A custom workflow (pasted in a drawing preset's card in Settings) is filled by placeholders (`{{prompt}} {{negative}} {{checkpoint}} {{width}} {{height}} {{count}} {{seed}} {{prefix}}`, and for image to image `{{image}} {{denoise}}`), or, with none, by finding the usual nodes. A custom workflow with no placeholders is started from a picture by swapping its `EmptyLatentImage` for LoadImage → VAEEncode → RepeatLatentBatch and setting every numeric `denoise` input; it needs a VAE (a `VAEDecode`'s link or a `CheckpointLoaderSimple`). `describeWorkflow(...).image` says whether a workflow can. To redraw only a painted area, `PictureParams.mask` is the name of a mask PNG in ComfyUI's input folder (same size as the picture, white = redraw): the built-in `BUILTIN_INPAINT` and a placeholder-less workflow add LoadImageMask → SetLatentNoiseMask before the sampler and paste the new pictures over the original outside the mask (InvertMask, ImageCompositeMasked) before saving; a placeholder workflow needs `{{mask}}` in its own LoadImageMask, or filling it throws. `describeWorkflow(...).mask` says whether a workflow can. **Reference pictures** (`src/lib/comfy/references.ts`: `Reference` = `StoredReference` plus `key`, `thumb` and `blob`, made from a `StartPicture` by `asReference`; at most 4; default weights character 0.4, item 0.4, place 0.3, style 0.8; character, item and place references stop at half the steps, so the model's own style comes back, a style reference runs to the end): `JobInput.refs` and `JobInput.ipAdapter` (the preset's base's `IpAdapterCheck`, from `drawPresetIpAdapter(editModels, draw)`, as `modelChecksFor` finds it with the other checks). `queueJob` uploads each reference once per key and ComfyUI address (`storytime-ref-….png`, `uploadReference`), then `fillWorkflow` (`PictureParams.refs` and `.ipAdapter`) calls `withReferences` (`ipAdapter.ts`) on the filled workflow, after the LoRAs: an `IPAdapterModelLoader` (`ref-loader`) and `CLIPVisionLoader` (`ref-clip-vision`), and per reference a `LoadImage` (`ref-N-image`) and an `IPAdapterAdvanced` (`ref-N`; weight type linear, “style transfer” for a style; concat, start 0, end 0.8, or 1 for a style; embeds scaling V only) chained from the sampler's model, which every `KSampler`/`KSamplerAdvanced` then takes from the end of the chain. This works for the built-in workflows and pasted ones without placeholders (`describeWorkflow(...).refs`); a placeholder workflow or a model family preset says it can't, and a job with references fails with a message naming what IP-Adapter is missing (`ipAdapterProblem`) rather than going without. An edit takes up to two references (Kontext one) as `EditParams.refs`: Qwen-Image-Edit's two `TextEncodeQwenImageEditPlus` nodes get them as `image2`/`image3`, Kontext a second chained `ReferenceLatent` (LoadImage → ImageScale → VAEEncode); an edit needs no IP-Adapter. With references, `framedForRefs` (`editModels.ts`) puts a sentence before the instruction saying image 1 (Kontext: the first picture) is the one edited and the others only show what to take from; without it the models blend the starting picture into the reference. `sameJob` treats different references (key, role, weight) as a different job. `ComfyGenerate` takes `refs`/`onRefs`/`refChoices` props from the prompt panel (see **The References section**). A model family's workflow takes the mask the same way (`buildDraw` calls `redrawOnlyMask`, pasting back the scaled picture, node "11").
- **The References section** (`References.tsx`, rendered by `ComfyGenerate` right above the Edit section, `#picture-references`; the same on the chapter and cover pages). Each chosen reference (up to 4) is a thumbnail with a label box, a Role menu (Character, Item, Place, Style; a new role also brings its usual strength unless the author had moved it), a Strength slider 0 to 1 (Low and High at its ends) and ✕ (“Remove reference <label>”). With none it says what references are for. **Add reference** opens the picker (a modal `<dialog>`, `ReferencePicker`) with three tabs: **Suggested** (making a footer: this chapter's header; making a header: the previous chapter's footer and header on the path, then this chapter's footer; on the cover page: the saved cover; only saved pictures that exist), **Pictures** (this page's pinned pictures, the headers and footers on the path latest first, the cover) and **Upload** (a file, scaled down to 1024 px; its source is `upload:<name>`). Choosing one adds it as a Character labelled “Header”, “Footer”, “Cover”, “Ch. <title> footer” or “Picture”, closes the dialog, and skips a picture already chosen (same source); at 4 the button and the choices are off, with a note. **Use as reference** in every picture's ☰ menu (`startItems`, after Change part of this…; `StartRequest` `"reference"`, which `usePictureWorkspace.startFrom` turns into a `refRequest` for the prompt panel instead of a starting picture) adds it the same way and scrolls to the section. Which pictures are offered comes from `referenceChoices` (`src/lib/comfy/referenceChoices.ts`) and the page's `RefPool`: the saved headers and footers of the chapters on the path and the cover, with their versions, read on the server (`src/lib/referencePool.ts`, `chapterRefPool`/`coverRefPool`, passed to the workspace as `pool`; the pinned pictures come from the workspace). When the drawing preset can't use references (a model family, a workflow with placeholders, or IP-Adapter not ready for its base: `ipAdapterProblem`, `describeWorkflow(...).refs`) the section is only its heading, the reason and a link to Settings, and no references are sent (they are kept, and come back with a usable preset). On the Instruct tab no IP-Adapter is needed; Qwen uses the first 2 and Kontext the first 1, and a note says so when more are chosen. The lists are held by `useReferences` (one list per group: the chapter's two moments, or the cover), which turns kept references back into pictures (`restoreReference`: `saved:<chapter or story id>:<slot>` through the pool's current versions, `comfy:<path>` through ComfyUI's address; one whose picture is gone is left out). **Kept** with the prompt boxes: `picture_prompts.refs` and `cover_prompts.refs` (migration 24, JSON `StoredReference[]`), sent through `usePictureDraft`/`useCoverDraft` with the same 0.7 s pause (`PictureBoxes.refs`, `CoverDraft.refs`; the draft APIs take and return `refs`, at most 4). An upload is never kept (its picture is gone with the page): it is dropped on load and left out of what is saved. **Load prompt** (from a picture's menu or Info) replaces the shown moment's references with the picture's own (`PromptToLoad.refs`, uploads skipped); a saved header or footer, which doesn't know its references, leaves them alone. Test: `tests/e2e/illustrate-refs.e2e.mjs`.
- **The Edit section** (in `ComfyGenerate`, `.edit-panel`, above Model Settings) holds a starting picture's controls: the preview on top, then the Redraw and Instruct tabs (`StartMode` `"edit"` is the Instruct tab; the ☰ menu still says "Edit this"), then Stop editing at the bottom. With no starting picture it is collapsed (heading only) and the tabs are off. Strength sliders have Low and High at their ends. Redraw has a **Redraw prompt (optional)** box: when it has text that text is sent as typed (no preset or Always include text added; the negative is unchanged), and Copy main prompt fills it with the main prompt as it would be sent, Always include text and all. It is not kept anywhere. What to show and Always include are growing multi-line boxes, Always include being below the Prompt box. **LoRAs** (the Instruct panel, for the edit preset, and Model Settings, for the drawing preset; `LoraToggles`): each of the preset's LoRAs has an on/off box, its name and a strength box (a number box with spinner arrows, `Strength of <name>`, step 0.05, -4 to 4), with a Change link to the preset in Settings. What is changed here is **only for this page and never written to the preset**: the changes are overrides (`src/lib/comfy/loraOverrides.ts`: by preset id, then the LoRA's place in the preset's stack plus its file name, so an override whose LoRA was changed or moved in Settings is ignored) applied over the preset's stack (`applyLoraOverrides`), and that effective stack is what the job uses (and `sameJob` compares). They are kept in this browser per chapter, cover or codex entry (`readLoras`/`writeLoras`, `editingState.ts`) like the instruction text. A **Reset LoRAs to preset** button shows next to a list only while the page has changes for that preset.
- **Painting an area to redraw** (Redraw only; an Instruct ignores it). `MaskEditor` (`src/components/MaskEditor.tsx`, helpers in `src/lib/mask.ts`) is a full-screen `<dialog>` (one finger, pen or mouse paints; two fingers pinch and pan) that gives back the strokes. It opens from the **Paint area** button in the starting-picture box (Redraw tab), from Change painted area, or from **Change part of this…** in the ☰ menu (right after Redraw from this: `StartRequest` `"paint"` = a Redraw start that asks the box to open the editor; the viewer and Info close first). The area is `PaintedArea` (`mask.ts`: strokes, softness, inverted, the picture's size, an `id` that changes with it), held by `usePictureWorkspace` next to the starting picture (`mask`, `setMask`, `paintNonce`) and kept with it in browser storage (`StoredStart.mask`, `editingState.ts`): only the strokes are kept, and the PNG is drawn from them (`renderMask`) when a job needs it, so a refresh brings it back. Choosing another starting picture, or Clear starting picture, drops it. The box tints it over the thumbnail (`useMaskPreview`, `StartMask.tsx`), says “Redrawing only the painted area (N%)”, and offers Change painted area and Clear painted area; Paint area is off, with the reason as its title, when the preset's workflow can't do masks. On the Edit tab a note says it is only used by Redraw for now. `queueJob` uploads the mask PNG as `storytime-mask-….png` beside the starting picture (once per mask, `uploadMask`), checks it is the picture's size, and passes `mask` to `fillWorkflow`; `sameJob` includes the mask's `id`, so a changed area starts a new job.
- **The job log** (`picture_jobs`; `src/lib/pictureJobs.ts`; `/api/picture-jobs`). One row per finished job: prompt, negative, checkpoint, size, seed, count, `moment`, `source` (`comfy:<subfolder/file>` or `saved:<chapter id>:<slot>`, null for text-to-image), `denoise`, `mask` (migration 22: the mask's file name in ComfyUI's input folder when only a painted part was redrawn, null for the whole picture; it stays there like the starting picture, and is in the job's `JobRecord`/tag), `refs` (migration 23: a JSON list of up to four `StoredReference`s `{source, label, role, weight}`, the reference pictures the job was made with, null for none; in the `JobRecord`/tag too, returned by GET, carried into `Picture.refs`, shown by Info as “References: Jim (character 0.4), Tavern (place 0.3)”, handed back in `PromptToLoad.refs` for Load prompt, and part of what `timeline()` groups by), and `images` (ComfyUI `{filename, subfolder, type}`). A cover job has no chapter and the moment `cover` (migration 20 rebuilt the table for it; it is recorded with `story_id` instead of `chapter_id`). `GET ?story=` (optionally `&not_chapter=`, which leaves the cover's jobs out too, or `&chapters=1`), `?chapter=` or `?cover=<story id>` lists newest first, `limit` 1 to 200, and `next` is passed back as `before`; each job has `marks`, one per picture (`pinned`, `rejected` or null), from `picture_marks` (migration 13), set with `PUT /api/picture-jobs/:id/mark {index, mark}`. The Illustrate page loads the chapter's latest 1000 jobs with it (`chapterPictureJobs`). The pictures stay in ComfyUI and are shown through its `/view`, so a lost file shows "ComfyUI no longer has this picture".
- **The prompt boxes are kept per chapter and moment** (`picture_prompts`, migration 14, which replaced migration 12's `picture_drafts`; `src/lib/pictureDrafts.ts`; `GET/PUT /api/chapters/:id/picture-draft`): prompt, negative, scene, What to show and the References (`refs`, migration 24), one set for Chapter start and one for Chapter end, so switching the "Generate Prompt From" menu (Chapter Start / Chapter End) brings each one's prompt back. The moment saved last is the one the page opens on (choosing a moment saves it). `PUT` takes one moment's boxes; `GET` gives `{moment, start, end}`. The page loads them with the chapter; `usePictureDraft` saves 0.7 s after typing stops, at once when the moment is switched, and sends a change still waiting (with `keepalive`) on `pagehide` or when the panel unmounts. Saves go one after another so the last one decides the moment. Not in the JSON export.
- **Text added to prompts.** A drawing preset has an optional `prompt` and `negative` (Settings › Drawing, “Preset prompt” / “Preset negative prompt”), and a chapter has an “Always include (this chapter)” box on the Illustrate page (`chapters.picture_fixed`, migration 21; `GET/PUT /api/chapters/:id/picture-fixed`, saved 0.7 s after typing stops and on `pagehide`). None of it is put into the prompt boxes: `ComfyGenerate` adds it when a picture is asked for (`combinePrompts` in `src/lib/ai/imagePrompt.ts`), in the order preset prompt, always-include text, prompt box; the preset's negative follows the negative box. Tag prompts are joined with commas without repeating a tag already there (case, underscores, brackets and `:1.2` weights ignored); sentence prompts skip a sentence the text already contains. The text sent is what the job log keeps, so Load prompt brings back the combined text (harmless for tags). Edits (Edit tab) get none of it. The preset's text is shown under the Prompt and Negative prompt boxes (`.prompt-art-style`), on the cover page too; the always-include text is cover-less. When the AI writes a prompt, it is told the always-include and preset text (`ImagePromptOptions.fixed`) and tags it repeated anyway are removed from its reply (`withoutGiven`, tags style only; the preset's negative is removed from the written negative the same way).
- **A saved header or footer** keeps the prompt and negative prompt that made it (`chapter_images.job_id/prompt/negative`, copied from the job by `PUT .../images/:slot?job=<id>`). Uploads keep none. The job log and `job_id` are not in the JSON export. A picture saved with its job and index is also **pinned** by the server in the same request (`setChapterImage` calls `setPictureMark`, which also lifts a trash mark), so it stays in Pinned & new; `usePictureSaver` is told which picture (`onPinned`) so the page's copy of its mark follows. Replacing or removing the header or footer later doesn't unpin the old picture. An upload, or a picture whose job couldn't be recorded, has nothing to pin. A saved **cover** works the same way (`stories.cover_job_id/cover_image_index/cover_prompt/cover_negative`, `PUT /api/stories/:id/cover?job=&index=`, `saveCover` and `coverMade` in `src/lib/coverPictures.ts`).
- **Jobs are tagged in ComfyUI:** `extra_data.storytime = {chapterId, storyId, clientId, job, instance}` (`JobTag`; `chapterId` null and moment `cover` for a cover job, whose pictures are filed under `StoryTime/<story id>/cover`). `instance` is this StoryTime's own id (`src/lib/instance.ts`, kept in the `settings` table under `instance_id`, from `GET /api/instance`), because **ComfyUI may be shared** with other StoryTimes and with tools that copy a job's tag. The picture queue's scan of ComfyUI only picks up a job when its `instance` is this one's, or it has none (made before jobs carried one) *and* its chapter exists here (`chapters` in the answer of `POST /api/picture-jobs/known {prompt_ids, chapter_ids}`); the others are ignored, so they are never shown, recorded, or stopped from here. A job whose record is refused with 404 (its chapter was deleted meanwhile) is dropped without a warning. A database copied from another StoryTime has the same chapter ids, so older jobs (without `instance`) for them can't be told apart; new ones can. Saved pictures are filed under `StoryTime/<story id>/<chapter id>` where the workflow's prefix is `StoryTime` or contains `{{prefix}}`.
- **Keep seed** (`ComfyGenerate`, under the settings, above Queue): ticked, every queue uses the seed in the box (filled with a random one, or typed by hand, a whole number; New seed re-rolls it) instead of a new one, and it goes into `JobInput.seed` ahead of Reuse seed. Queues of the same prompt and seed still join one job, and the pictures of one batch differ from each other (ComfyUI makes each batch item's noise from the one seed), so the batch size isn't limited; only a separate later job with the same everything repeats earlier pictures.
- **Export workflow** buttons on each drawing and edit preset card (`exportWorkflow.ts`) download the API-format workflow StoryTime would send, with a sample prompt or instruction (an edit loads `example.png` from ComfyUI's input folder), for trying in ComfyUI by hand. A model-family or edit preset needs Check setup to have found its files first.
- **Settings** (`settings.comfy`, in `src/lib/settingsSchema.ts`): url, width, height, `coverWidth` and `coverHeight` (the cover page's size), `drawPresets`, `editPresets` and `editPreset` (the edit preset last used). A drawing preset holds a `family` ("checkpoint", the default and what presets saved before families parse as, or a model family), a checkpoint (the "checkpoint" family's, and what `{{checkpoint}}` is in a pasted workflow), `files` (by part key, "" or missing = found by name) and `textEncoderDevice` for the other families, LoRAs, Redraw strength, sampler settings (`Partial<DrawTuning>`: steps, CFG, sampler, scheduler and, for the families that have them, `shift` and `guidance`) and an optional workflow. A "checkpoint" preset also has an optional `base` ("sdxl", "illustrious", "noobai" or "pony"; missing = `guessBase` of the checkpoint's file name, `src/lib/comfy/ipAdapter.ts`), set by the Model base select in Settings; it picks the IP-Adapter files for reference pictures, and the setup check reports them per preset as `DrawCheck.ipAdapter` (informational only: a preset without IP-Adapter is fully usable; jobs that carry references need it, see Workflows). Choosing a family in Settings sets the preset's `promptStyle` to sentences and clears its `sampler` overrides; the Advanced settings start from the family's defaults (`DrawModel.defaults`). The setup check reports a family preset per preset (`DrawCheck.family`, a `FamilyCheck`: parts, missing nodes and options, `ggufNeeded`), and the Illustrate page gets the same checks as `EditModels.families` (`drawPresetFamily`), which `ComfyGenerate` hands to `queueJob` as `JobInput.family` (null for a checkpoint preset or one with a workflow of its own); the job log's checkpoint column holds the family's model file (`madeWithText`). An edit preset holds one edit model's files, LoRAs, Fast box (set in Settings only; the Illustrate page has no Fast box, and an edit is fast when the preset says so and ComfyUI has the speed-up file), `textEncoderDevice` ("default" or "cpu", the text encoder loader's `device`), default strength and sampler settings (`src/lib/comfy/presets.ts`). **Edit models and GGUF** (`src/lib/comfy/editModels.ts`): a model part's file can be a `.gguf` (needs ComfyUI-GGUF's `UnetLoaderGGUF`, `CLIPLoaderGGUF` or `DualCLIPLoaderGGUF`, a part's `gguf`). A part's choices are its normal loader's list plus the GGUF loader's, and the GGUF nodes are needed (for `ready`) only when a `.gguf` file is in use; the check carries what `/object_info` says about each loader (`EditCheck.loaders`, `ggufMissing`, `devices`). `buildEdit` switches the loader node of a part whose file is `.gguf` to the GGUF loader and keeps only the inputs that loader lists (so `weight_dtype` goes), and drops `device` when the text encoder loader in use doesn't list it or the value; LoRA chains are unchanged. A safetensors file is preferred to a GGUF one when both match. Settings marks `.gguf` choices "(GGUF)" and says to install ComfyUI-GGUF when its nodes are absent. Test: `tests/e2e/illustrate-gguf.e2e.mjs` (the fake ComfyUI's `gguf: true`); the drawing families': `tests/unit/draw-models.test.mts` and `tests/e2e/illustrate-families.e2e.mjs`. They are shared across stories and held on the server. (`count` and the top-level `imagePromptStyle` are left over and unused.)
- **The story's picture settings** (`StoryPictureSettings` on the story's details page, `#pictures`): art style (`stories.art_style`) and drawing preset (`stories.draw_preset`, migration 18; null follows the first preset). A chapter can choose another preset (`chapters.draw_preset`, null follows the story's), from the Model Settings panel (next to its Preset menu is a gear link to the preset in Settings), whose Drawing preset menu marks the story's preset "(default)". They keep a story's pictures alike, so they are the story's, not the chapter's or the app's. `stories.checkpoint` (migration 16) is only read once, to turn the checkpoints stories used into presets. The chapter's Illustrate page does not show them (the art style is added to prompts, and the preset is in Model Settings); the cover prompt panel still shows them as one small muted line (`StoryPicturesLine`). **The prompt style** (tags or sentences) is no longer the story's: it is `promptStyle` on each drawing preset (Settings › Drawing), and prompts are written in the style of the chapter's preset, else the story's. `stories.image_prompt_style` is left over and unused. The prompt panel's write button is an icon (sparkle, then a redo arrow once a prompt was written) beside the eye button; its accessible names stay "Write prompt" / "Write again". The page title is `Illustrate “<chapter title>”`; the `IllustrateChapter` panel is headed "Chapter Text" (no title line), with its Summary and the whole chapter and Story so far each in a fold that starts closed and is remembered in the browser. While a prompt is being written the write button becomes a Stop button (same size, `StopIcon`). The Pictures panel has no visible title: its view buttons are on the left of one line, and the order and Clear unpinned buttons are on the right. They are in the JSON export and import. **The story's style picture** (`stories.style_ref`, migration 26: a JSON `StoredReference` with role "style", null for none; `PATCH /api/stories/:id` takes `style_ref`, role must be style and weight 0 to 1; not in the JSON export, since the pictures it names are tied to ids that change on import): the “Style picture (optional)” part of the `#pictures` card has a thumbnail, Choose… (the `ReferencePicker` from `References.tsx` with `noUpload`: Suggested, Pictures and Codex from the cover's pool, since an upload can't be kept), a Strength slider (0.8 to start with) and Remove, saved with Save picture settings. The Illustrate, cover and codex pages get it as `story.style_ref` and hand `ComfyGenerate` a `styleRef` (`restoreReference`; a picture that is gone, or not in the page's pool, is left out): the References section shows it first as a fixed “Story style” row (role Style, its strength, set on the details page) with a **Use the story's style picture** box, ticked by default, and kept in this browser per page and moment (`localStorage`, `storytime:style-ref-off:<target>:<moment>`, not on the server). While ticked it counts toward the 4 references and goes first in the job's references (weight type “style transfer”), and is in the job log like any reference; it is not shown or sent on the Instruct tab, and a reference of the same picture already in the list stops it being sent twice. Test: `tests/e2e/story-style-ref.e2e.mjs`.
- **A story can have no chapters yet** (New story makes it from its title, summary and style notes). Its details page then shows Start writing (`/s/<id>/write`, draft key `first:<story id>`, posted with `POST /api/stories/<id>/first-chapter`); once it has a chapter, Read.

- **Freeing Ollama's memory for a picture job (the mirror):** an Ollama connection can have `freeForPictures` ("Unload this server's models before each picture job" in Settings; shown for Ollama connections only). `queueJob` calls its `beforeQueue` hook once per job (a job of several pictures counts once; drawing, redraw, edit and cover jobs alike), after the job is checked and its starting picture uploaded and just before `POST /prompt`; the picture queue passes `freeOllamaForPictures` (`src/lib/ai/ollama.ts`), which for every ticked connection does `GET <root>/api/ps` and then `POST <root>/api/generate {model, keep_alive: 0}` for each loaded model, from the browser (needs `OLLAMA_ORIGINS`). `<root>` is the connection's address without a trailing `/v1`. One 3 s timeout for all of it, failures ignored: the job is sent anyway. Not yet tried against a real Ollama. Test: `tests/e2e/free-for-pictures.e2e.mjs`.
